# Bookmarks

Bookmarks are a *way to let your brain take a break and close the book without the stress of remembering the page number every time they do so*.

As one said,
!!! quote
    Le banana, le bahaga.  
    Montserrat lemonyo,  
    Bookmarks are importanto.    
    I need to take a breako.  
    Di legaya, hajajajaja.

## `BookmarkError`

!!! Info "Main page"
    *Main page:* [BookmarkError](../../errors/BookmarkError.md)

A bookmark error is what happens when you don't pay enough attention at what you're doing.  

Placing bookmarks outside the book will never make sense.[^1]

[^1]: It will in fact make sense after the book grows out of its hardcover.
