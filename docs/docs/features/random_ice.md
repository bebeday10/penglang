## Target
Some had made a target to bomb jenguins.  
These targets could be found in a matter of seconds, or millenniums.  
It all depended on how big the targets were.  
### Detailed versions
After a while, penguins realized that they wouldn't know the progress of the target finding.  
So, even though it was slower, they made a very big progress bar.  
Then they added more stuff.  
But performance decreased very little!
## The end
In the end, penguins had developed many innovative things, and jenguins couldn't catch up.  
Many bombs were shot at the jenguins.  
The jenguins *surrendered*,  
and life became peace again.
