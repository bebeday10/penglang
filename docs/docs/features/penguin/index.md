## A story about a penguin
One time in a faraway [crowd](../pengcrowd/index.md), a penguin [dances](../output/dance.md).  
They proceeded to go on a roundabout.  
They went left with no pain[^2].

But then, a different *penguin* went right,  
And a painful tsunami came by.  

Everyone sweeshed and swashed.

Even the ones that went left in the whole tsunami.  

??? question "When and why were there tsunamis in such a peaceful island?"
    When the penguin went right.[^3]
