# Fish
Fish are foods for penguins to eat. They are also living water creatures under the sea.  

In *PengLang*, fish can swim *both ways* after innovation by *Pengnology*™[^1].

Fish can swim at a record-breaking *30* swim ticks per second.  

!!! tip "Fun fact"
    Fish favor the *right* way.
    ??? question "What happened to the left?"
        Whenever they went left, they encountered a wall.

[^1]: Swimming is cool.
