# Bananas

*PengLang* has a thing known as bananas.  
They are nutritious for the average penguin[^1].  
Penguins eat them because they are "cool".

## The Banana How-To
### What They Have
- name: Clearly, penguins need to check their banana names.

    ??? question "I'm in a predicament. Why do they have names?"
        Because bananas are very important to name.  
        Or else, how would you call your banana?  
        If they said "Banana!", who knows what will happen.  

- size: Used to check the BMI.
- weight: Stronger the better! Mainly for fighting other bananas.

    !!! note
        They are also used to prevent sweesh swashing.  

- freshness: Make sure they aren't rotten.
- potassium: What's the point of eating a banana if there isn't any potassium?
- species: The type of banana.
  
    !!! note
        These are very different from names. Beware!  
        ??? quote "A quote from a penguin"
            Oh me oh,  
            I fo a lo,  
            My names got mixed up,  
            Ended up a trial of bananas,  
            Help!

- peeled, eaten: Status of the banana

### What They Can Do
- **peel**: Peel the banana.
- **eat**: Eat the banana.
- **throw**: Throw the banana.

??? warning "Sequences"
    To eat the banana, you must peel the banana.  
    To throw the banana, you must eat the banana[^2].

- get potassium: Check how potassiumized the banana is.
- bmi: Get the BMI. Formula: `weight ÷ size²`
- get weight category: Some penguins go on a diet. Lighter the more diet-friendly!
  - 0-1: tiny banana
  - 1-3: normal banana
  - 3-5: big banana
  - \>5: chonky banana

- quality ratio: How rich the banana is. Used to check the best bananas.
  - Formula: (Potassium * Freshness) / BMI * 50

[^1]: They are a hazard if they aren't average.
    ??? question "I have fed them a banana, what do I do?"
        You can't do anything but watch them.  
        We hope we could bring better news.

[^2]: A penguin once tried throwing before eating.  
    It ended up that the banana flew and caused a massive earthquake.  
    Didn't go so well.
