# PenguinKaraokeMachine

Penguins wanted to sing songs so they made a machine that let them do it without the music singing in the way.  

After making song designs the penguins wanted to actually run them.

## History

According to literate sources[^1], the penguin who held a talk about the song merger also made this karaoke machine.  
They said:
!!! quote
    Hey everyone!   
    Say *heeey*,  
    Say *hoooe*!  
    Meet the person of the *karaoke*!  
    *Jazz music plays in the background*  
    Every time I don't drop CDs,  
    I always make sure to play them...    
    But when I wanted to sing along...  
    I don't know what they said (*Help!*),  
    So I made a machine,  
    that let me put my own lyrics!  
    I don't actually know what the song is saying,    
    but I get the vibe of it.

[^1]: We can confirm that most sources are literate and not made by a baby penguin.
