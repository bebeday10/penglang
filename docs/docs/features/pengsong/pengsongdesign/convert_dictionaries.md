# Convert dictionaries
Imagine waking up one day finding your dictionary being found one day and converted into lyrics for a song. That would be awesome![^1]

!!! tip "*Pro* tip"
    Ever had a duplicate in your dictionary?
    Just put `|` after your lyrics and put a number!  
    ??? example
        `"banana|2"`  
        If you have a 3rd lyric called banana.

[^1]: According to this one penguin that has had this happen to them before, it isn't really awesome. You'll end up listening every word from A to Z. Makes penguins get psycho!
