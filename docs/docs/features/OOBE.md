# OOBE

**PengLang** has a **OOBE (Out-Of-Box Experience)**.  
It gets installed when you install **PengLang**.

??? question "Sorry, what?"
    An OOBE is normally an initial setup process.   
    In PengLang, it is used to "setup the penguins".

## Naming scheme

??? question "Scheme? Pyramid..."
    No.

The naming scheme of the OOBE (ooh-beh) is "Penguin Box Of Stuff" (peng-hu-ween boh-ks ove sht-uhffe) or the PBOS (pee-boast).

## Startup

It starts when one makes a PBOS. Things like "Language/Region", "Let's connect you to the PengNet", "Set up your Penguin account" appear.  

## Get out!
When one calls, "Get something!" or the PBOS itself, it randomly chooses one of the things in it and takes it out of the PBOS.   
They can also choose more than one at the same time.  
If **you** get chosen, *get ready to experience the OOBE*.  

What happens:

- It gets out of the box
- You are no longer part of the box
- You are not counted in the length of the box
- The box no longer has you
- If you are the only one, and you got pulled, the box is now wrong.

## What to do

Live life outside the box. Be free.

## What happens to the OOBE

It has you removed.  
After that, if there are no more, the PBOS will shout at the maker when they try to take another thing.
